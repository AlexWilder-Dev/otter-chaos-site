# Otter Chaos: research notes

Gathered 18 October 2026, before any design. Sources: otterchaos.co.uk (home, events, food & drink, games, booking, T&Cs, and the
WordPress media library via its public `wp-json/wp/v2/media` endpoint), the April 2026 food & drink menu PDF, their DesignMyNight
venue and gift card pages, Meetup (the three groups that run their nights), Ticket Tailor, their Disney Lorcana store profile
(Ravensburger play locator), BoardGameGeek (user `Otter_Chaos`), Brixton Buzz, and TikTok's profile data.

## The place

- **Two railway arches** on Atlantic Road, directly under Brixton rail station. Opened in early 2024 in Arch 22 (Brixton Buzz,
  19 March 2024, “recently opening up in the beleaguered railway arches”; their TikTok was already saying “London’s newest
  boardgame cafe coming soon!!” in September 2023). Brixton Buzz (Feb 2026) says they’ve “recently expanded to take in two arches”.
- **Run by three friends**: “We are a boardgame cafe in the heart of Brixton, run by 3 friends” (their Lorcana store profile).
- **Inside** (from their photos): a white-painted corrugated barrel vault with warm fairy lights strung across it in loops, long
  strip lights, and aircon units bolted to the curve. One long wall is painted **deep bottle green** (sampled ≈ #014D3E) with a
  big chalkboard (“Welcome to Otter Chaos”, kitchen hours, wifi) and an otter drawn on it. **Floor-to-ceiling birch-ply shelving**
  packed with game boxes runs along the walls and up into the curve, with plushies and framed prints on top. Plywood booths with
  maroon cushions down one side, butcher-block tables (≈ #E2B075) on black hairpin legs with black metal café chairs, polished
  concrete floor. A white door marked TOILET.
- **The bar arch** is painted orange/ochre on the curve. Pipe-and-plank back bar with spirits and glasses, two fridges of cans,
  a coffee grinder, brownies under a cake dome, Mondo taps (Hiker lager), Otter Chaos T-shirts and tote bags hung up for sale,
  pink Otter Chaos pint glasses. A plush otter holding a fish appears in their drinks shoot.
- **Outside**: a brick railway arch with graffiti, a glass front with “pizza · cocktails · board games” lettered on the window, a
  pink-and-blue otter sticker (“Otter Chaos / Brixton’s Boardgame Café”), teal tiles at the side. At night the lit sign reads
  “Otter Chaos” and a chalk A-board says **“Board Games + Beer On Tap + Pizza”**.
- **Trains overhead**: they’re literally under the station; their own line is “directly underneath Brixton rail station and
  just a dice throw away from the tube”.
- DesignMyNight lists **wheelchair access and disabled toilets**, and “Background Music”. One of their own photos shows a
  wheelchair user at a D&D table.

## Brand: logo, type, colour (from their files and CSS)

- **Logo**: a cartoon otter floating on its back hugging an **orange d6** with red-orange pips. Browns #4E3B2F (outline) and
  #835E4B (body), cream belly, die #F18C45, pips #D05731. A second version adds a big “OC” in brown and orange on a pale disc.
- **Site CSS** (WordPress “Ember Dawn” block theme): background #FFF8EF, text #30271C, accents #F28C45 / #ED8802, browns #4E3B2F and
  #835E4B: their web palette *is* the logo palette. Body font **Host Grotesk** (Google, OFL). Headings in **Clemente PD**
  (Prescott Design, 2003, “All rights reserved”, embedding flag = preview/print), an Avant Garde-style geometric with the joined
  “tt” you see on their shop sign and in “Otter”.
- **Menu PDF** fonts: Questa Slab headings, Blue Highway item names, Belgiano Serif tasting notes, Cochin Bold cocktails. Every drink
  carries a **card suit** (♠ lager / sparkling, ♣ IPA / white wine, ♥ cider / rosé / mead, ♦ alcohol-free / red), and tasting
  notes are separated by tiny ♥s.
- The DesignMyNight booking widget is themed teal #6DA8A6, which appears nowhere else.

## Voice

Warm, chatty, a bit cheeky, lots of exclamation marks, and the odd kaomoji: “Followed by an Otter Chaos afterparty! (>ᴗ•)”.
Lines worth keeping verbatim:

- “Nestled under Brixton station, Otter Chaos has over 500 games for you to play.”
- “Bring your family, a couple of friends, or the whole gang for a wonderful time playing anything from the silliest of party
  games, to the most involved heavy strategy games.”
- “We also host a range of events, in case your friends are boring and don’t want to play games with you every evening.”
  (Brixton Buzz, 2024, quoting them)
- “We *always* recommend you book ahead.” (the “always” is styled on their page)
- “just a dice throw away from the tube”
- “Grab a drink. Roll the dice. Shape the story.”
- “Rock up on a Tuesday night…”, “make sure to sign up adventurer!”
- Pizzas are puns on games: Cheese Monopoly, Taco Cat, Pass on the Pigs, That’s my Fish!, Exploding Chickens, Pepperoni for
  Neanderthals.

## Who comes when

- **Weekend daytime (Sat/Sun from 11am)**: families and friends (“bring your family”, children’s table fee, hot chocolate with
  marshmallows), plus the Saturday French/Spanish language socials and Sunday Shut Up & Write sessions.
- **Weekday evenings (from 5pm, Friday from 2pm)**: after-work groups and dates (DesignMyNight files them under “Quirky Activity,
  Date Night, Chilling with mates”), pizza and the cocktail two-for deal.
- **Solo social nights**: Tuesday Board Game Social Mixer (“come alone or bring friends”); staff check you in and seat you with a group.
- **The hobby crowd**: Disney Lorcana organised play (Mon), Blood on the Clocktower with OverBoard (Wed), D&D with Dungeon Masters
  (Thu), one-offs like the It’s Not Chai Tea Carrom Social and The Drinking Dungeon Halloween show.
- **Parties, clubs and brands**: private parties, tournaments, corporate events and “full brand takeovers” (Big Potato Games’ Switch
  Off, Game On, 22–23 Jan 2026; a Lambeth & Croydon Foodbank fundraiser, Apr 2026; New Year’s Eve party poster on the bar).
- Review themes (DesignMyNight, 5 reviews, all 5★): staff help pick and teach games, lots of new games discovered, cosy and
  “a more intimate and cheaper alternative to some of the other board game cafes in London”. Tripadvisor search snippets (pages
  themselves blocked) echo it: titles like “A great asset to Brixton!”, “Best Board Game Cafe in London (and the cheapest I’ve
  been to)”. No complaints were reachable; Google reviews couldn’t be read.

## What’s weak on the current site

1. **Booking** is a DesignMyNight widget dropped mid-page; the table fee sits under it, with no total for your group.
2. **Open now?** Hours are a static list; the kitchen’s weekend 4–5:30pm gap is easy to miss.
3. **Events**: the weekly nights sit under a heading that says “One Off Events”; a past event lingers; the summary strip says
   Lorcana starts at 7pm while the listing says 6:30pm; and **every Meetup link points at a single past occurrence** (e.g. the
   Tuesday link is 18 Aug 2026, Clocktower 19 Aug, D&D 20 Aug, Shut Up & Write 12 Jul). Lorcana has no sign-up route at all.
4. **Menu** is only a PDF whose text layer is jumbled; the game-pun pizzas and suit-marked drinks are invisible to phones and search.
5. **Games** page is two sentences and a link out to geekgroup.app.
6. **Private hire** is one sentence buried on the events page. Their media library shows they started building a hire section in
   April 2026 (uploaded “individuals / clubs / corporations” icons and the Eon Next takeover photo) that never went live.
7. **Gift cards** exist on DesignMyNight (£10–£50, 12 months, spendable on table fees, food, drink and games for sale) but aren’t
   mentioned on their own site.
8. **Link preview** is bare: no og:image or description, so a WhatsApp share shows just a URL.

## Games library

- Their BoardGameGeek account is `Otter_Chaos` (joined Sept 2024). BGG's XML API now needs a token and its web pages are behind
  Cloudflare, so the owned list came from geekgroup.app’s mirror of the collection (`POST api.geekgroup.app/api/users/collection.json`):
  **520 owned games plus 17 expansions** (research/games-full-list.json), which matches their “over 500”.
- Players, play time and BGG weight for 191 of those came from BoardGameGeek’s own JSON API (api.geekdo.com geekitems and
  dynamicinfo). research/games.json is the 90-game sample used on the site: 34 casual party/icebreakers, 16 co-operative, 40 strategy.
- All six games the pizzas are named after are on their shelves: Monopoly, Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza, Pass the Pigs, Hey, That’s
  My Fish!, Exploding Kittens and Poetry for Neanderthals. Their shelves also hold Splendor and Heat (both in their own photos).

## Design reasoning

- **Palette** is the logo, then the room: cream #FFF8EF and ink #30271C from their CSS; otter browns; the die’s orange for every
  primary action (it’s the one thing the otter is holding); bottle green from the wall for the “what’s on” board, like their
  chalkboard; birch-ply tones for shelves; brick red only on the arch frames.
- **Type**: Host Grotesk for body, as on their site. Headings use **TeX Gyre Adventor**, a freely licensed cut of the same Avant
  Garde Gothic design as their Clemente PD wordmark and shop sign. Clemente PD is “All rights reserved” with restricted embedding,
  so it isn’t redistributed in this repo; it can be swapped in at handover if their licence covers web use.
- **Layout** comes from the arches: photos sit inside a brick railway-arch frame with a festoon of fairy lights that shiver every
  so often as if a train has gone over. Shelves become the games library (box spines on birch shelves). The menu’s card suits mark
  drinks and sections, and the gift card is drawn as a playing card.
- **Phone first**: the first screen answers open-now (café and kitchen separately), what’s on tonight (or what’s next), how to
  book, and what the table fee is. A bottom bar keeps Book one thumb away.
- **Signature interactions**: (1) a swipeable fortnight strip, today first, every night with its event, price and real sign-up
  route, honest about quiet nights; (2) a game picker (players on the logo’s die, time, party / co-op / strategy) that pulls real
  boxes from their BGG collection and ends at “Book a table for N” with the fee worked out. Pizza cards flip to show the game each
  one is named after.

## What the owners most want this site to do

1. Fill tables: make booking one tap from anywhere, with open-now and the table fee impossible to miss.
2. Fill the week: every regular night, monthly night and one-off in one place, with the right sign-up link and nothing stale.
3. Turn the second arch into income: private hire, clubs, corporate takeovers and gift cards as enquiries and sales.
